Global Data Professional Survey Dashboard (Power BI)

## Business Goal
To transform raw survey responses from data professionals into a comprehensive, interactive dashboard that businesses can use to benchmark salaries, understand market demand for specific roles, and identify future hiring trends.

## Key Findings
The dashboard provides immediate answers to critical HR and strategic questions:

* **Salary Benchmarking:** The `Average Salary Distribution by Data Role` chart clearly shows **[Data Scientist/Data Engineer]** as the highest paying role.
* **Language Demand:** The visualization on `Preferred Programming Languages` confirms that **[Python and SQL]** are the top two required skills for the industry.
* **Experience vs. Pay:** An analysis of experience levels revealed that the largest salary increase occurs between the **[X-5 years]** and **[5-10 years]** experience bands.

## Methodology
Data was loaded and cleaned using Power Query to handle text inconsistencies. Custom DAX measures were then calculated to derive average salaries and key performance indicators (KPIs). The final report was designed for clarity and published using the **Publish to Web** service.
